# Interview Prep Prompt

Use this prompt with the skill:

Act as the hiring manager for this role and interview me based on my CV.

Target role:
[role title]

Job description:
[paste job description]

My CV:
[paste CV]

Please produce:
- The top 10 questions I am likely to be asked.
- Technical deep-dive questions based on my strongest claims.
- Questions that test weak areas or missing requirements.
- STAR answer outlines using only the experience shown in my CV.
- Claims I must be ready to defend.
- A final prep checklist.
