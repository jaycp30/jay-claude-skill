# CV/Resume Review Rubric

Score the CV out of 100 across these categories:

## 1. Role Alignment — 25 points

- 21-25: Clearly targeted to the role; headline, summary, skills, and bullets all reinforce fit.
- 16-20: Mostly targeted, but some generic or underemphasised areas.
- 10-15: Relevant experience exists, but the CV reads like a general history.
- 0-9: Weak alignment or unclear target role.

## 2. Evidence and Impact — 25 points

- 21-25: Strong metrics, clear outcomes, scale, ownership, and business/technical impact.
- 16-20: Some quantified achievements, but uneven.
- 10-15: Mostly responsibilities with limited measurable evidence.
- 0-9: Vague claims with little proof.

## 3. Keyword and ATS Fit — 20 points

- 17-20: Strong natural keyword coverage and ATS-friendly structure.
- 13-16: Good coverage with a few missing terms.
- 8-12: Important keywords missing or formatting may cause parsing issues.
- 0-7: Poor keyword coverage or likely ATS parsing problems.

## 4. Readability and Structure — 15 points

- 13-15: Easy to scan, clear sections, strong ordering, concise bullets.
- 9-12: Generally readable but too dense or uneven.
- 5-8: Hard to scan, long bullets, unclear hierarchy.
- 0-4: Disorganised or confusing.

## 5. Credibility and Risk — 15 points

- 13-15: Claims are credible, dates are clear, seniority is supported.
- 9-12: Minor questions or unsupported claims.
- 5-8: Several recruiter concerns.
- 0-4: Major credibility risks.

## Score Interpretation

- 90-100: Excellent. Minor polish only.
- 75-89: Strong. Good chance if targeted properly.
- 60-74: Usable but needs meaningful rewrite.
- 40-59: Weak. Needs repositioning and stronger proof.
- Below 40: Not competitive for this role yet.
