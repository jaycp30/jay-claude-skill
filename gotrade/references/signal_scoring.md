# Signal Scoring

Use this reference to make the Buy / Hold / Sell research signal mix consistent.
The signal mix is not a personalized recommendation. It is a compact summary of
research evidence and uncertainty.

## Scoring Principles

- The three percentages must add to 100%.
- Do not let one positive data point dominate serious red flags.
- Reflect uncertainty with a higher Hold percentage.
- Strong fundamentals can support Buy, but stretched valuation should reduce it.
- Bad price action alone is not a Sell signal unless the business thesis changed.
- Analyst ratings are context, not a decision engine.
- If information is stale or conflicting, use "Unclear" in the written sections
  and avoid extreme scores.

## Default Score Anchors

Strong positive setup: 70 Buy / 20 Hold / 10 Sell
- Fundamentals are solid.
- Recent news is positive or de-risking.
- Analyst context is broadly bullish.
- Valuation is reasonable for growth and quality.
- No major balance-sheet, legal, or execution red flags.

Moderate positive setup: 50 Buy / 30 Hold / 20 Sell
- Fundamentals are intact.
- News is mixed but not thesis-breaking.
- Pullback appears tied to normal volatility or broad market pressure.
- Valuation is fair or only moderately stretched.

Neutral setup: 20 Buy / 60 Hold / 20 Sell
- Mixed evidence, unclear catalyst, or fair valuation.
- Good business but no obvious margin of safety.
- Recent move is hard to explain from public information.

Caution setup: 10 Buy / 30 Hold / 60 Sell
- Negative news, weakening fundamentals, deteriorating guidance, or valuation
  that leaves little room for error.
- Analyst sentiment is deteriorating or estimates are being cut.

Strong avoid setup: 5 Buy / 15 Hold / 80 Sell
- Serious red flags, thesis break, liquidity concerns, heavy dilution, major
  legal/regulatory risk, dividend cut risk, or business deterioration.

## Score Caps

Cap Buy at 50 when:
- Valuation is clearly expensive.
- Data is incomplete or stale.
- Earnings are within the next few days and the user is not explicitly trading
  the event.
- The company is unprofitable with high cash burn.

Cap Buy at 30 when:
- There is a credible thesis break.
- Debt, dilution, or liquidity risk is material.
- Management guidance was sharply reduced.
- A major legal/regulatory issue could impair the business.

Keep Hold at 50 or higher when:
- The user already owns the stock and the thesis is intact but valuation is
  stretched.
- The signal is mostly "wait for more data."
- Recent price action is noisy and not tied to fundamentals.

## Written Summary Pattern

Use one sentence like:

"Research signal mix: moderate positive, but position sizing matters because the
portfolio already has meaningful AI/semiconductor exposure."

Avoid:
- "You should buy."
- "This will go up."
- "Guaranteed upside."
- "No-brainer."
