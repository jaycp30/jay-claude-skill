## REITs / Real Estate Income

Examples in portfolio: ADC, ARR, EQIX, O, STAG.

Research prompts:
- Is the REIT equity, mortgage, industrial, retail, net lease, or data center?
- What is funds from operations (FFO/AFFO) trend?
- Is the dividend covered by AFFO or at risk?
- How sensitive is the business to interest rates and refinancing?
- What is occupancy, lease duration, tenant quality, and debt maturity profile?
- For mortgage REITs, check book value, leverage, spread income, and rate risk.

Common red flags:
- Dividend yield rising because price is falling.
- AFFO payout ratio too high.
- Heavy near-term debt maturities.
- External management or high leverage.
- Tenant concentration or falling occupancy.

## Mega-Cap Internet, E-Commerce, And Cloud

Examples in portfolio: AMZN, GOOGL.

Research prompts:
- What is driving growth: ads, cloud, commerce, subscriptions, or AI?
- Are cloud margins expanding or compressing?
- Is capex rising because of AI infrastructure, and is revenue following?
- Are regulatory, antitrust, privacy, or app-store/search risks material?
- Is valuation supported by free cash flow and operating margin trends?

Common red flags:
- Slowing core growth while capex accelerates.
- Regulatory remedies that could alter the business model.
- Margin pressure hidden by one fast-growing segment.
- Expensive valuation despite estimate cuts.

## Semiconductors And Semiconductor Equipment

Examples in portfolio: ASML, INTC, LITE, MU, NVDA, TSM, TXN.

Research prompts:
- Is demand driven by AI, memory cycle, industrial/auto, PCs, smartphones, or
  data centers?
- Are revenue and margins cyclical or structurally improving?
- What does inventory look like across customers and channels?
- Are export controls, China/Taiwan risk, or supply-chain constraints material?
- For fabs/foundries, check capex intensity, utilization, yields, and node
  leadership.
- For equipment suppliers, check order backlog, lithography demand, and customer
  concentration.

Common red flags:
- Peak-cycle earnings being valued as permanent.
- Customer concentration in one hyperscaler or geography.
- Inventory correction or order cancellations.
- Gross margin pressure from pricing, yields, or underutilization.
- Geopolitical risk not reflected in valuation.

## AI/Data-Center Networking, Power, And Infrastructure

Examples in portfolio: ANET, VRT, EQIX, NVDA, TSM, CEG, GEV.

Research prompts:
- Is growth tied to durable AI infrastructure demand or a temporary buildout?
- Are hyperscaler orders broad-based or concentrated in a few customers?
- Are power constraints, grid interconnection, cooling, or data-center capacity
  bottlenecks affecting growth?
- Is backlog converting to revenue at healthy margins?
- Does valuation already assume several years of flawless execution?

Common red flags:
- AI narrative running ahead of signed orders and cash flow.
- Margin pressure from supply-chain constraints.
- One or two customers driving most incremental demand.
- Capital spending boom with uncertain returns.
- High multiple plus slowing order growth.

## Power, Utilities, And Electrification

Examples in portfolio: BE, CEG, GEV, NEE.

Research prompts:
- Is demand linked to data centers, electrification, renewables, nuclear, grid
  upgrades, or hydrogen/fuel cells?
- Are earnings regulated, contracted, merchant, or project-based?
- What are capex needs, debt levels, and interest-rate sensitivity?
- Are tax credits, subsidies, or policy changes important?
- For clean-energy names, is the path to profitability credible?

Common red flags:
- High debt and rising financing costs.
- Project delays, cost overruns, or warranty issues.
- Reliance on subsidies or policy support.
- Negative free cash flow with repeated capital raises.
- Commodity or power-price exposure misunderstood as stable utility income.

## Aerospace, Defense, And Space

Examples in portfolio: LMT, PL, RDW.

Research prompts:
- Is revenue government, commercial, subscription, project, or milestone based?
- What is backlog quality and contract duration?
- Are margins stable, improving, or pressured by fixed-price contracts?
- Are budgets, procurement cycles, or geopolitical events affecting demand?
- For space companies, check cash burn, dilution risk, launch/customer
  dependence, and path to profitability.

Common red flags:
- Contract delays or cancellations.
- High cash burn and repeated dilution.
- Revenue concentration in one agency or customer.
- Technical execution risk.
- Backlog that is nonbinding or low margin.

## Energy / Oil And Gas

Example in portfolio: XOM.

Research prompts:
- What are oil and natural gas price assumptions?
- Are earnings driven by upstream, downstream, chemicals, or trading?
- Is free cash flow covering dividends and buybacks through the cycle?
- What is the breakeven oil price?
- How much capex is needed to sustain production?

Common red flags:
- Valuation based on peak commodity prices.
- Dividend/buyback commitments unsupported at lower oil prices.
- Large acquisitions increasing integration or balance-sheet risk.
- Regulatory, climate, or litigation risk.

## Telecom / Connectivity

Example in portfolio: VZ.

Research prompts:
- Are wireless subscribers growing or shrinking?
- Is average revenue per user improving?
- Is churn under control?
- How heavy is debt, and when does it mature?
- Is dividend coverage sustainable after capex and spectrum costs?

Common red flags:
- High leverage plus weak growth.
- Dividend yield rising because the stock is under pressure.
- Price competition hurting margins.
- Heavy capex with limited revenue growth.

## ETFs And Diversified Exposure

Examples in portfolio: VIG, VXUS.

Research prompts:
- What index does the ETF track?
- What are the top holdings and sector exposures?
- Does the ETF overlap with existing single-stock positions?
- What is the expense ratio, dividend yield, and geographic exposure?
- Is the ETF being used for diversification or duplicating the same factor bet?

Common red flags:
- False diversification from overlapping mega-cap holdings.
- Currency/geographic risk not considered.
- Dividend focus that sacrifices total return.
- Using ETFs and single stocks to unintentionally double up on the same theme.
